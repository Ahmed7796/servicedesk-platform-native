## Decisions made
- `approverUserIds` sits on `currentStep` / `nextStep` (an `actionableStep`), not on every entry of `steps[]`, because only the step now awaiting a decision is resolved. Alternative: on every step; rejected because later steps' approvers could change before they are reached.
- `request.submitted` carries both `steps[]` (all steps, after escalation) and `currentStep` (the first, with IDs). Small duplication, but consumers need not cross-reference.
- Producer schemas are strict (`additionalProperties: false`); consumers are lenient (README rule 2). Strict schemas catch misspelled field names in the API's tests.
- No `description` in `request.submitted` (up to 4000 chars); the copilot reads it over HTTP.
- `$id`s use `servicedesk.example`, a reserved domain, so they don't look like a real host.
- `comment` on approval is `string | null`; `reason` on rejection is required, matching `Reject`.
